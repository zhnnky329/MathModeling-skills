# Task-Type Guide

Classify by what must be produced and defended.

- **Evaluation/ranking**: score, rank, grade, priority, or comparative assessment. Validate indicator meaning, direction, weighting, differentiation, and rank stability.
- **Prediction/estimation**: future or unknown value, interval, or missing value. Validate split design, leakage, error, uncertainty, and extrapolation.
- **Optimization/decision**: plan, allocation, route, schedule, maximum/minimum, or feasible policy. Validate variables, objective, constraints, feasibility, and implementability.
- **Mechanism/dynamics**: explain how a process evolves or interacts. Validate units, parameters, boundary/initial conditions, conservation, and fit to observations.
- **Classification/clustering**: assign labels or groups. Validate label availability, imbalance, separation, stability, and appropriate metrics.
- **Graph/routing/network**: paths, flows, matching, or connectivity. Validate graph abstraction, edge semantics, operational constraints, and route feasibility.
- **Simulation/scenario**: distributions or policy outcomes under uncertainty/repetition. Validate distributions, seeds, replications, intervals, and scenario assumptions.
- **Descriptive/inference**: patterns, factors, associations, or tested hypotheses. Validate data quality, uncertainty, multiple testing, and correlation/causation boundaries.

A task is mixed only when one output genuinely feeds another modeling structure, not merely because several analytical steps are present.
